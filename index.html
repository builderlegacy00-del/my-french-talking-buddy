🎤 Important for microphone practice:
Use the downloaded HTML file directly in Chrome. Do not use the ChatGPT Library preview for microphone practice. The preview can place the page in a restricted browsing context, where browser speech recognition may return “not-allowed” even when device microphone permission is enabled.

Quick test: tap 🎙️ Test microphone first. If it says microphone access is working, then tap 🎤 Speak.

How Stage 1 works:
The app uses your browser's built-in French speech voice and speech-recognition feature where supported. It does not require a TalkPal subscription or a paid language-learning platform. Speech recognition support varies by browser, so typing is also available.

The practice material is drawn from your existing useful-phrases, diacritics, 16-verb, and multiple-meaning-noun workbooks. Stage 1 gives simple feedback; a later version can add deeper AI-style grammar correction and more natural open conversation.
