Stage 4.0.5 tutor mode
The Buddy now acts as a more context-aware French tutor and can also run immersive role-play: it checks your answer first, explains a genuine mistake in simple English, gives the corrected French, lets you hear it, and waits for you to repeat or type the correction. Once the correction is accepted, the Buddy automatically continues with a connected follow-up and shows the English meaning of that actual question. It also keeps your corrected idea in the conversation, so the next question is based on what you really said. The correction detector works with normalized text, so missing accents or punctuation will not hide a known correction. It still separates grammar correctness from conversation fit, supports present/past/future tense coaching, and accepts multiple natural answers.

Learning rule: your answer does not have to match one exact sentence. Different natural French answers can be correct. When Buddy corrects you, learn the correction first; after you confirm it, Buddy continues with a translated, connected question.

Privacy note: conversation memory and unfinished answers are saved locally on this device so you can leave and return later; they are cleared when you choose 🧹 New conversation. This remains a free browser-based version. Stage 4 uses browser-side adaptive rules and your current-session learning data; it is not yet a full cloud AI tutor.

Stage 4.0.3 adaptive coaching: Buddy now keeps a small session coach record, varies repeated follow-up questions, highlights useful vocabulary from what you actually say, shows a simple session progress summary, and gives a short coaching tip after accepted answers. The goal is to make the practice feel less repetitive while keeping the learner in control of the conversation.
